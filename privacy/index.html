Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information on coryfosco.com.

Effective Date: June 7, 2026

Who we are

This site is operated by Campion Hall Press LLC, an Illinois company. Cory Fosco is the author. You can reach us anytime at cory@coryfosco.com.

What we collect

We collect two kinds of information:

  • Your name and email when you sign up for the Four Signal Categories checklist through our MailerLite form.
  • Automatic site analytics through Google Analytics 4 (Measurement ID G-CFS89629LS), including page views, device type, referring source, and general region.

How we use it

We use your information to:

  • Deliver the checklist you requested.
  • Send a short welcome sequence and occasional updates about The Question of When.
  • Analyze site traffic so we can improve the content.

Third-party services

We rely on a few trusted providers, each with its own privacy policy:

  • MailerLite for email storage and delivery.
  • Google Analytics 4 for site analytics.
  • Vercel for site hosting.

Your rights

  • You can unsubscribe using the link in every email.
  • You can email cory@coryfosco.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
  • You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser opt-out add-on.

Cookies

Google Analytics sets analytics cookies (_ga and _gid). The Meta Pixel may also set cookies to measure ad performance, as described in the next section.

Advertising and the Meta Pixel

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook and Instagram). It collects information about your visit, such as the pages you view, your device and browser information, and your IP address, and may use cookies to do so. We use this to measure the performance of our advertising and to show relevant ads to people who have visited the site. You can manage your ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings and control cookies through your browser. For more, see Meta's Privacy Policy.

Children

This site and the related email list are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

EU and UK legal basis

We rely on your consent for the email signup, and on legitimate interest for analytics and site security.

US state privacy rights

Residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, and Utah have the right to know what personal information we hold, to access it, to delete it, and to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The Effective Date above will reflect the latest update. Material changes are flagged on the site and emailed to current subscribers.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email cory@coryfosco.com.
