About the author

Written from inside the room

Waiting feels like love. Acting feels like giving up. Neither is true, but by the time most families learn that, the decision has already been made for them.

Cory sat across the admissions desk from hundreds of families who waited too long, not because they did not care, but because nobody told them what to look for or when to move. This book is the conversation he had with each of them, written down for the adult child watching a parent change, and for the spouse managing alone and wondering how much longer.
