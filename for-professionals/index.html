A practical guide your team can hand to families at the moment of decision.

The Question of When helps families understand assisted living, memory care, and skilled nursing before they're in crisis. Written by a veteran of the long-term care industry. Built for families. Designed to be shared by the professionals who serve them.

Built for the moment families walk in unprepared.

Every hospital discharge planner, admissions director, home health intake coordinator, geriatric care manager, and elder law attorney has had the same conversation hundreds of times. A family arrives confused about the difference between assisted living and memory care. They don't know what Medicare covers. They don't know what questions to ask on a tour. They've never been here before, and they're making a decision on a timeline that doesn't give them room to learn.

The Question of When is the book you put in their hands. It answers the questions your team spends time answering every day. It gets families to the decision point with a shared vocabulary and a clearer head. It doesn't replace your team. It makes your team's time more valuable.

How institutions use this book

Hospitals & Health Systems

For case management, discharge planning, and transitional care teams. Hand a copy to families facing post-acute placement. Reduce repeat questions and support readmission-reduction goals with better-prepared families.

Senior Living & Skilled Nursing Operators

For admissions, sales, and family-engagement teams. Pre-admission reading that gets families to the tour with a clearer understanding of what they're evaluating. Positions your team as educators, not salespeople.

Home Health & Hospice Agencies

For intake coordinators and clinical liaisons. Helps families decide honestly whether home care is enough, and when it is, sets them up to succeed with your agency instead of second-guessing themselves.

Geriatric Care Managers & Aging Life Care Professionals

For Aging Life Care Association members and independent care managers. A companion resource for the families you're already guiding. Reinforces your framework and saves time covering the same ground twice.

Elder Law Attorneys & Estate Planners

For NAELA members and life-care planning practices. Pairs with the legal and financial work your clients are already navigating. Helps them arrive at the care conversation with the same clarity you built on the legal side.

Senior Placement & Advisory Services

For independent placement advisors and senior care consultants. A value-add for the client families you serve, and a resource that helps them arrive at your conversations better prepared.

Long-Term Care Insurance Carriers

For member services, claims, and education teams. A benefit-eligibility companion that helps policyholders make smarter decisions with their coverage. Fewer confused calls. Better claim satisfaction.

Area Agencies on Aging & Caregiver Nonprofits

For caregiver support programs and family-advisor roles. An evergreen resource that holds up across years of caregiver cohorts, from a 34-year industry veteran.

Thirty-four years inside long-term care.

Cory Fosco has spent his career in the industry families turn to at the hardest moments of their lives. He started in direct service through the Jesuit Volunteer Corps Southwest and the Mesa Senior Center, trained as a social worker, and has spent more than three decades working inside skilled nursing, assisted living, post-acute, and the technology that connects them.

The Question of When was written for families because Cory has watched too many of them arrive at the decision without the guidance they needed. The book is not about any one company or any one community. It's about families, and the professionals who serve them.
